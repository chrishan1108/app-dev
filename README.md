**Ashin of the North**
#
***Kingdom: Ashin of the North*** is a dark, atmospheric special episode that serves as both a prequel and side story to the Korean historical horror series Kingdom.
term
: definition
Set in the northern border region of Joseon-era Korea, the story follows Ashin, a young woman from a marginalized tribe who experiences devastating personal tragedy. After her village is destroyed and her family is killed, Ashin grows up consumed by grief and a desire for revenge. Her journey is deeply tied to the mysterious “resurrection plant,” a herb capable of bringing the dead back to life—but with horrifying consequences.
